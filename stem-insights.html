Submission Guidelines

Read carefully before submitting. Articles that don't meet our standards will not be published.

✅

Original Content Only

Every word must be your own. Your article must be entirely original and not published anywhere else online or in print.

🚫

No AI-Generated Text

Articles written or assisted by AI tools (ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.) will be immediately rejected. We verify using detection tools.

❌

Zero Plagiarism

All submissions are checked with plagiarism detection software. Any copied content — even paraphrased — leads to disqualification.

📏

Length & Format

Articles must be between 600–2000 words. Use clear headings, simple language, and reference any sources cited.

🎯

Relevant Topics

Content must be directly relevant to STEM education, admissions in Pakistan, or career guidance for students.

🌟

Quality Writing

Articles should be well-structured, factually accurate, and genuinely helpful to fellow students. We value depth over length.
