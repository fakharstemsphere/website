From Classroom to Code: How Pakistani Students Can Start Their Tech Journey Early

By Hifza Emaan · Class 10 Student & Founder of Emaan Tech

Introduction

Technology has become an important part of modern education, communication, and professional life. For students in Pakistan, learning digital skills can provide opportunities to explore their interests and understand the world of technology.

However, many students are unsure where to begin. Some believe that programming requires expensive equipment or advanced knowledge. In reality, beginners can start with basic concepts, accessible learning resources, and small projects.

In this article, I will explore how students can begin learning technology while continuing their school education, develop practical skills, and gradually discover opportunities in the digital world.

My Journey into Technology

How I Started Learning Web Development

I joined the web development course associated with Saylani Mass IT Training at my school, Bahria College. I joined it almost randomly, but these 12 months changed my perspective — how websites are built, and how data is stored on the internet.

Exploring New Skills and Opportunities

Alongside my course, I did some further courses at HP LIFE and attended a summer camp held at NUST PNEC University covering digital marketing, CAD, and robotics.

Balancing School and Technology

Sometimes I get frustrated when I have pending school tasks, exams, and coding assignments all at once. But I understood that both are equally important, so I practiced coding at night and did my school tasks during the day. Any skill can be developed if you have interest and dedication.

Managing exams alongside the course became harder, but my hard work paid off. Every student should start working hard to achieve their dreams. Mine was to become a full-stack developer. After completing my course I felt very grateful: I had learned how to make websites, and then I made one myself.

Inspiration Behind Emaan Tech

I was inspired by Kashan Adnan, a 13-year-old who built his own company, and I asked myself: why can't I? So I started my startup, Emaan Tech by Hifza, where we teach students web development, and more courses are coming. We go beyond lectures and YouTube tutorials, with real live examples and hands-on experience. I also launched a website at emaantechbyhifza.pages.dev.

Learning Through Practice

Can AI Replace Human Skills?

People think AI can replace us. I believe no AI can compete with us, and we have to win that competition.

A Lesson I Learned from an Assignment

I once had to make a music player as part of my web and app course. I thought AI could do it, so I made it with AI and submitted it. My teacher knew I hadn't made it myself. He explained that AI can do a lot, but not better than humans; you can use it, but you should know how to use it and you should practice by yourself.

Finding the Right Balance Between AI and Learning

Many students think AI can replace us, but that's not the truth. Every field has scope when we know how to learn.

A Teacher's Words of Encouragement

I remember the golden words my mentor said in the last class of our course:

"Yahan pe jab ye course shuru hua tha tou 75 bache the magar unme se Hifza ki consistency is great. Hifza ne is opportunity se faida uthaya and I believe she will do it inshallah."

These words felt real to me, and I started again with more dedication, thinking that Sir Asif believes in me, so it can happen. Many students demotivate themselves, but we should always stay motivated because failure is a part of learning.

Conclusion

Learning from Failure

The most important thing is to never be scared of failure. I think there is little time to quit the course and study only for school, but skills matter more than a degree.

Encouraging Students to Start Their Tech Journey

Every student should take the initiative and begin their journey in tech. Don't think that technology can harm you: "every technology is good until you are good."

About the Author

Hifza Emaan is a Class 10 student, Frontend Developer, and Founder & CEO of Emaan Tech. She is passionate about web development and technology, and helps students explore digital skills through practical learning.
